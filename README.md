# Edge

## Overview

Edge provides an agent-based approach to assist businesses in optimizing their operations and decision-making processes.

## Purpose

This project leverages intelligent agents to help organizations:

- **Streamline Operations**: Automate and enhance business workflows for improved efficiency
- **Support Decision-Making**: Provide data-driven insights and recommendations
- **Optimize Resources**: Utilize operational capabilities more effectively

## Approach

Edge employs agent-based systems that work intelligently to understand business needs and deliver targeted assistance across various operational domains.
